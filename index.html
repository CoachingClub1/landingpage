Honest answers

What people worry about

I'm really unfit. Will I embarrass myself?

No. Most people start exactly where you are. Session one is mostly talking and gentle movement, in a private studio — no queues, no mirrors full of gym bros, nobody watching.

I've failed at this before. Why would this be different?

Because last time nobody was watching. We fix the actual reason it stalled — no structure and no accountability once motivation dipped — rather than asking you to want it more.

I genuinely don't have time.

Two hours a week. An NHS doctor on 12-hour shifts and a bloke running three businesses both found it. We build around your diary, not the other way round.

Do I have to give up the food I love?

No. No banned foods, no meal plan you'll abandon. Mark lost 28kg and still has a beer. We change the smallest number of things that actually move the needle.

I'm in my 50s/60s. Am I too old?

No. We coach every age and ability, working around dodgy knees, backs and shoulders. Progress at your pace beats intensity you can't sustain.

What does it cost, and am I tied in?

From £160 a month for weekly 1-2-1 sessions. We'll find the right package on your free call — and you'll know the exact number before you commit to anything.

What actually happens on the free call?

Twenty minutes. I ask what you've tried, what your week looks like and what you want to be able to do. You ask me anything. Then we both decide — no follow-up spam either way.
